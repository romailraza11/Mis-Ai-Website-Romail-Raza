Romail Raza

I learned that Management Information Systems connects technology, information, and human decision-making in a way that influences how organizations work, communicate, and grow. Before this project, I mainly thought of technology as a communication or entertainment tool, but now I understand that information systems are central to how businesses and institutions solve problems. MIS is about more than software or hardware because it includes how data is collected, stored, protected, and used to create better outcomes. This assignment helped me see why organized information matters in both academic and professional settings. I also learned that good digital systems are built to support people, not just to process information quickly. My understanding of MIS grew because I had to connect technology, ethics, and real-world decision-making.

One of the most interesting parts of this project was learning about AI and its ethical boundaries in college and the workplace. AI can be useful for brainstorming, explanation, and practice, but it should not replace a student's effort or critical thinking. I learned that using AI responsibly means checking facts, following class rules, and understanding the final work well enough to explain it clearly. This is important because AI can generate confident but incorrect information, which is risky in both school and professional settings. The biggest lesson is that technology is powerful when it is used with honesty and accountability. I now see that responsible AI use is not only about efficiency, but also about integrity and judgment.

The cybersecurity section also made a strong impression on me because it demonstrated how quickly a weak security habit can create major problems for organizations and communities. I learned that ransomware, malware, and weak passwords can expose personal or business data and disrupt essential services. Simple actions such as updating software, using multi-factor authentication, and backing up information can make a meaningful difference. These lessons showed me that cybersecurity is practical, preventive, and necessary for daily life in a digital society. I also learned that protecting information is not only a technical challenge, but a responsibility shared by everyone using technology. This made me more aware of how important digital safety is in modern life.

Overall, this assignment showed me that technology is most valuable when it is used ethically, critically, and thoughtfully. MIS helps people understand how information guides decisions, AI demonstrates how innovation can support learning when used responsibly, and cybersecurity reminds us that digital systems require care and vigilance. This project strengthened my awareness of academic integrity, digital safety, and informed judgment. I am grateful for the opportunity to explore these topics because they have helped me understand how technology shapes society and why responsibility matters as much as innovation. This experience gave me a stronger appreciation for ethical technology use in school and in the future workforce.
