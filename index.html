Management Information Systems is the field that connects people, technology, data, and decision-making so organizations can operate effectively and make better strategic choices. In business and education, information systems help people collect, organize, and use data to solve problems, improve communication, and support long-term planning. My project helped me understand that MIS is not just about computers or software; it is about how information moves through an organization and how people apply it responsibly. It also showed me that technology becomes valuable when it is used with purpose, ethics, and critical thinking. Gartner explains how technology trends influence decision-making, while MIT Technology Review highlights how digital change affects society and business. I now see that understanding MIS is essential for anyone who wants to work with data, systems, or modern organizations.

AI and cybersecurity are closely connected to MIS because they influence how people work, learn, and protect information in digital environments. Artificial intelligence can help students and professionals process information faster, but it must be used with verification, honesty, and ethical judgment. AI can produce helpful explanations, but it can also generate incorrect or incomplete results when it is trusted without review. Cybersecurity is just as important because weak habits, poor updates, or unsafe online behavior can expose data and disrupt essential systems. Through this assignment, I learned that responsible technology use requires both knowledge and accountability. Cisco provides useful guidance on digital security, and the NIST AI page explains why trustworthy AI requires transparency, testing, and careful use.
