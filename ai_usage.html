Correct AI Usage

My reflection: Artificial intelligence can be a valuable academic tool when used responsibly and ethically. Students can use AI to help with a multitude of difficult concepts, generate study guides, brainstorm ideas, improve grammar, and receive feedback on drafts. AI also helps students organize information, review material before exams, and discover additional learning resources. However, students should think on their own, research concepts that interest them in depth, and analyze rather than relying solely on AI. Responsible AI use means verifying information for accuracy and following the course and professor's policies regarding its usage. AI's goal is to support learning, not replace critical thinking or personal development. Students using AI honestly can improve productivity while also bettering their own personal skills and remaining ethical. Ethical AI is necessary for students to build knowledge and skills that will benefit them in future careers.

Benefits of AI

  1. Explains difficult ideas in clearer language and different formats.
  2. Generates study questions, notes, and revision prompts that encourage deeper engagement.
  3. Helps students brainstorm and refine ideas before drafting academic work.
  4. Supports efficient review and self-assessment when paired with independent reasoning.

Who Benefits?

  1. Students who use AI responsibly to strengthen understanding and develop better study habits.
  2. Teachers who use AI as a support tool for lesson planning, feedback, and clearer communication.
  3. Professionals who combine AI capabilities with human judgment, ethics, and expertise.

How AI Tools Are Used Responsibly

  • Summarizing readings or class notes to improve understanding.
  • Checking grammar, structure, and clarity while keeping the student's ideas intact.
  • Generating practice questions that encourage review and deeper learning.
  • Helping students compare perspectives and improve their ability to reason critically.

Incorrect AI Usage

Submitting AI work as your own
Copying an AI-generated answer and claiming it as original work violates academic honesty and prevents real learning from taking place.
Using fabricated information
Accepting AI output without checking accuracy may lead to misinformation, false citations, or weak academic reasoning.
Breaking course rules
Using AI in a way that an instructor prohibits, or failing to disclose required AI assistance, can be treated as misconduct.
Overreliance on automation
When students depend on AI for final thinking instead of developing their own understanding, they miss important opportunities to build professional and academic skills.

In my opinion, AI is valuable when it is used ethically and thoughtfully. It becomes a problem when a student uses it to avoid learning, to hide a lack of effort, or to submit work they do not truly understand. Students should take personal responsibility for the quality, honesty, and integrity of everything they submit. My view is that AI should be used to support learning and improve communication, not to replace thinking, creativity, or independent judgment. This way, technology becomes an extension of student effort rather than a substitute for it.
